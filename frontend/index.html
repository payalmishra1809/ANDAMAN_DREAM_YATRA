An island story, told at the pace of the tide

Weather & Seasons

Best time to visit Andaman

Andaman weather stays warm year-round, but the season you pick changes the seas, the crowds and what's in bloom. Here's what to expect, month by month.

January

23-29°C
Winter

The dry, mild winter season begins, with calm seas and comfortable daytime temperatures. It's one of the best months for island hopping.

February

23-30°C
Winter

Still firmly in the dry season, with pleasant temperatures and some of the clearest underwater visibility for diving and snorkelling.

March

23-32°C
Summer

Summer sets in with warmer days. Look out for Shivratri and Holi festivities around the islands this month.

April

25-33°C
Summer

The hottest month of the year, with high daytime heat, though mornings and evenings are still pleasant for sightseeing.

May

25-31°C
Pre-Monsoon

The rainy season typically begins, bringing frequent showers. Good time for fewer crowds if you don't mind occasional rain.

June

24-30°C
Monsoon

Monsoon season starts in earnest, with strong winds and regular but generally short spells of rain.

July

24-29°C
Monsoon

Consistent monsoon weather continues. Indoor sightseeing and museum visits work well between showers.

August

24-29°C
Monsoon

One of the wetter months, with heavier rainfall throughout, so ferries and water activities may see occasional delays.

September

24-29°C
Monsoon

Temperatures hold steady with monsoon conditions gradually easing as the month progresses.

October

24-30°C
Peak Season

Peak season begins with pleasant weather. Durga Puja, Dussehra and Diwali are celebrated across the islands.

November

24-30°C
Peak Season

Monsoons recede completely, leaving calm seas and clear skies, giving excellent conditions for every island activity.

December

24-29°C
Peak Season

The busiest month of the year, with Christmas and New Year celebrations drawing travellers from across the country.
