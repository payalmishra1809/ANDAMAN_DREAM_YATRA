Water Activity

Flyboarding

Flyboarding uses a hose connected to a jet ski to push water through boots or a board, lifting you up to several metres above the sea - part hovering, part diving, all adrenaline.

Why choose flyboarding?

It is among the most thrilling water sports on offer, and a trained instructor controls the throttle so beginners can fly within minutes. Best for confident swimmers who want something beyond the usual boat-and-beach activities.

Best spot for flyboarding in Andaman

Sessions are typically run at Corbyn's Cove near Port Blair and at Havelock (Swaraj Dweep), in calm morning conditions. Availability depends on weather and operator schedules.

What to bring

Swimwear and a towel; operators provide the helmet, life jacket and boots. Remove loose jewellery and secure glasses.

Experience overview

After a safety briefing and a short practice in the water, your instructor powers the jet ski and you rise above the surface. Sessions typically last 15-30 minutes including briefing.

Quick Facts

  • DurationApprox. 15-30 mins incl. briefing
  • Best forAdventure seekers
  • WhereCorbyn's Cove, Havelock
  • CertificationNone required; swimming ability advised
