Cancellation & Refunds

Please read this policy carefully before confirming a booking. It applies to all packages, stays, ferries, cruises and activities booked through Andaman Dream Yatra unless a specific booking states otherwise in writing.

1. Booking Confirmation & Advance Payment

A booking is confirmed only once the advance payment communicated at the time of enquiry is received. Advance amounts vary by season and package, and are shared in writing (email or WhatsApp) before payment is requested. Full payment is due as per the schedule confirmed in your booking voucher, generally no later than 15 days before travel.

2. Cancellation by the Traveller

Cancellation charges are calculated on the total package cost and depend on how far in advance we receive written notice of cancellation:

  • 30+ days before departure - 10% of total package cost deducted as cancellation charge; balance refunded.
  • 15–29 days before departure - 25% of total package cost deducted.
  • 7–14 days before departure - 50% of total package cost deducted.
  • 0–6 days before departure, or no-show - No refund; the full package cost is treated as forfeited.

Where a package includes flight tickets, hotel bookings, or ferry/cruise tickets that carry their own separate cancellation terms from the airline, hotel or operator, those third-party charges apply in addition to the slabs above and are non-negotiable on our end.

3. Cancellation by Andaman Dream Yatra / Force Majeure

Andaman & Nicobar travel is subject to tidal windows, monsoon weather, cyclonic warnings, ferry and flight schedules, and occasional government or Forest Department restrictions (for example around the Jarawa Reserve convoy or protected-area permits). If a trip, ferry, cruise or specific activity cannot proceed due to any of the above, or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control:

  • We will first attempt to reschedule the affected portion of your itinerary within your travel dates, or offer a suitable alternative of equal value.
  • If no alternative is workable, the cost of the specific unused service (not the entire package) is refunded, minus any charges already levied by third parties (airlines, ferries, hotels) that are non-recoverable.
  • Andaman Dream Yatra is not liable for costs arising from delays or cancellations caused by circumstances outside our control, including but not limited to weather, government orders, strikes, or natural disasters.

4. Refund Process & Timelines

Approved refunds are processed to the original mode of payment within 7–10 working days of the cancellation being confirmed in writing. Bank processing times beyond this may add a few additional days depending on your bank or payment provider. The following are non-refundable in all cases, as they are paid to third parties in advance on your behalf: government permits and park entry fees, visa/ID processing charges (where applicable), and any portion already utilised before cancellation (e.g. completed nights of a stay).

5. Amendments & Date Changes

Date changes and headcount changes are accommodated wherever possible, subject to availability of flights, ferries and hotels for the new dates:

  • Requests made 15+ days before original departure - one free date change per booking, subject to availability and any fare difference.
  • Requests made 7–14 days before departure - a rescheduling fee of 10% of package cost applies, in addition to any fare difference.
  • Requests made within 6 days of departure are treated as a cancellation under Section 2 and a fresh booking, as most components can no longer be amended at that notice.

Reducing group size after confirmation may change the per-person package price, since many components (private cabs, boats) are priced per group rather than per head; any resulting price adjustment will be shared before confirming the change.

6. Weather-Dependent Activities

Activities such as the Barren Island cruise, scuba diving, snorkelling, sea-walk and parasailing are conducted by certified local operators and are subject to sea and weather conditions on the day. If an operator cancels an activity on safety grounds, we will rebook it for another day within your itinerary where possible, or refund that activity's cost if no suitable slot remains.

7. How to Request a Cancellation or Change

All cancellation, refund or amendment requests must be sent in writing to andamandreamyatra@gmail.com with your booking reference, full name and travel dates. Verbal or phone requests should always be followed up in writing so we have a clear record and can process your request without delay.
